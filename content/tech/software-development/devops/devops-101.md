# Hi-tech, DevOps & JFrog 101

A slide deck for people who work with engineers but are not engineers themselves - HR, recruiting, sales, finance. It explains the words, the people and the process behind every app, without a single line of code, and then shows how one company's products (JFrog) fit into that picture.

## What's inside

**Part 1 - Hi-tech & DevOps 101** (slides 1-19)

1. **Hi-tech vocabulary** - where software lives (frontend, backend, cloud), how a tech team works (sprints, backlog, QA), programming words, technology names and what they are for (Python, Node.js, Java, Go, C/C++, Maven), and the business side (funding rounds, ARR, equity).
2. **What is DevOps?** - the road from idea to customer, the old wall between Dev and Ops, the DevOps loop, and keeping software running (monitoring, on-call, incidents).
3. **Code, packages & containers** - source code and Git, open-source packages, the build and its artifacts, containers and Kubernetes, and the software supply chain.
4. **Who's who** - the roles you meet, and a ten-word cheat sheet.

**Part 2 - JFrog 101** (slides 20-30)

A one-minute recap, the supply-chain problem, and then the products in plain language: Artifactory (the warehouse), Curation, Xray and Advanced Security (the inspectors), Distribution, Connect and JFrog ML (delivery), where each one sits on the road, and a JFrog cheat sheet.

Part 2 is unofficial learning material. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by JFrog Ltd., and product details may be out of date - for anything that matters, check [jfrog.com](https://jfrog.com).
