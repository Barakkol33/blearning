# About me

Simple sentences to talk about yourself:

- **I am** ten years old.
- **I have** a dog and a cat.
- **I like** pizza and football.
- **My name is** Noa.

To ask a friend, turn it into a question: *What is your name? How old are you? Do you like pizza?*

**Try it:** write three sentences about yourself.
