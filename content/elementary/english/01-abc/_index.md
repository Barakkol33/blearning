# The ABC

The English alphabet has 26 letters. Each letter has a capital form and a small form: **A a, B b, C c**.

- Names and the first word of a sentence start with a capital letter: *Dana lives in Haifa.*
- The letters **A, E, I, O, U** are vowels. All the others are consonants.

**Try it:** write your name in English, starting with a capital letter.
